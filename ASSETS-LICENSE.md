# Asset licence

The MIT licence in `LICENSE` covers the Lua source, scripts and text files in
this repository. This file covers the art: everything under `42/media/models_X/`, `42/media/textures/`, `42/media/sound/`, and the `icon.png` image.

You are welcome to:

- use and modify these assets in Project Zomboid mods, with credit and a link
  back to this repository;
- study them, take them apart, and learn from how they were made.

**Except the audio.** The reuse grant above covers the models, textures and
icon. It does **not** cover the clips in `42/media/sound/`, with one exception
noted below.

Most of the voice clips are derived from commercially licensed sound libraries.
That licence lets this mod use and ship them, in unlimited projects, free or
paid — but it forbids passing the sounds on to anyone else, in the licensor's
words: *"Licensee may not transfer, assign, share or sub-lease the licensed
sound effects with any other party."* Granting you reuse rights over them would
be exactly that, and it is not ours to grant.

This changes nothing about installing, playing or streaming the mod, and nothing
about the models and textures, which are ours. It means only that you cannot
lift the audio files out and use them in your own project.

**The exception is the squirrel**, whose clips derive from CC0 recordings and
carry no such restriction — reuse those freely. `CREDITS.md` lists exactly which
sources are which, per species and per clip.

If you want audio in the same register, the libraries are named in `CREDITS.md`
and you can license them yourself on the same terms we did.

Please don't:

- reupload this mod wholesale, to the Workshop or anywhere else — release links
  belong in one place so fixes actually reach players;
- sell these assets or include them in paid products.

Models and textures began as [Meshy](https://www.meshy.ai/) generations and were
substantially reworked — retopology, rigging to the game's own skeletons,
texture repair — before shipping.

Sound clips are derived from the sources listed in `CREDITS.md` — commercially
licensed libraries for the canids and felids, CC0 Freesound recordings for the
squirrel. The originals remain under their own terms; this notice covers only
the edited clips as shipped, and the audio exception above applies.

If you want to do something these terms don't cover, open an issue and ask —
the answer is probably yes.
